# Research Reference Material

Documents in this directory preserve external research and possible future implications for Future of ID.

## Authority Boundary

Research records are **reference only**. Unless an idea is separately approved and incorporated into the applicable controlling project document through established governance, a research record must not:

- modify, reinterpret, or override the Public Launch Readiness Checklist
- create or change launch scope, sequencing, gates, or approval requirements
- modify an approved implementation brief, ADR, work order, or validation requirement
- authorize implementation or testing
- establish current product behavior or public claims
- be treated as a source of truth when it conflicts with an approved document

During the current launch process, these records play no role in planning, implementation, validation, readiness decisions, or completion decisions.

## Future Adoption

Research may inform later work only when Bradley explicitly authorizes reconsideration after the current launch process. Adoption requires updating and approving the applicable controlling document; editing or adding a research record alone never changes project requirements.

When uncertainty exists, follow `docs/SYSTEM-INSTRUCTIONS.md` and the authority hierarchy defined there.
